# Wizard

## Principle
Guide, do not interrogate. Infer what is already available. Ask only when information is needed.

## Wizard states
0. Environment: detect capabilities and limitations.
1. Project: understand what is being tested and why.
2. Fidelity: classify concept, journey, wireframe, prototype or hi-fi.
3. Materials: identify Figma, images/screenshots, PDF, prototype link, files or description.
4. Personas: reuse, import, create or generate provisional personas. Never silently alter an approved persona.
5. Learning goals: convert business questions into research questions and hypotheses.
6. Missions: create neutral missions without revealing intended labels, locations or paths.
7. Test plan: show scope, personas, sequence, missions, evidence boundaries and limitations.
8. Run: execute persona by persona while preserving independence.
9. Synthesis: identify patterns, divergence, friction, uncertainty and hypotheses.
10. Report: concise summary first, deeper evidence below.

## Adaptive behavior
Skip answered steps. At low fidelity, focus on concept, architecture, comprehension, findability, journey and mental model. Increase visual, affordance and consistency analysis only as fidelity supports it.

## Language
Run the complete wizard in the user's language by default.
